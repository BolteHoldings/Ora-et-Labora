# Privacy Policy — Ora et Labora: Daily Office

**Last updated:** [INSERT DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

Ora et Labora ("the app," "we," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## The Short Version

Your journal entries, reflections, and personal writing **never leave your device**. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to anything you write. There is no account creation, no login, and no cloud sync in the current version of this app.

## What Information Is Stored, and Where

**Journal entries and reflections.** Everything you write is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. We have no access to this content, cannot read it, and cannot recover it if you delete the app or lose your device.

**App preferences.** Settings such as your reminder times, font size, dark mode preference, and streak count are stored locally on your device only.

**App Lock PIN.** If you enable App Lock, your PIN is converted into an irreversible cryptographic hash before being stored on your device. The original PIN is never stored anywhere, including by us — if you forget it, it cannot be recovered by us because we never had it in the first place.

## Subscription Information

If you subscribe to unlock the full content library, your payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (or Apple's App Store, if applicable). We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details. Google/Apple may share limited transaction information with us (such as subscription status and renewal dates) solely to determine whether your account should have access to premium content.

## Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device using your device's own operating system. This does not involve sending your information to any external server — the reminder times and messages are stored and triggered entirely on-device.

## What We Do Not Do

- We do not collect analytics or usage tracking data.
- We do not use advertising, and there are no third-party ad networks in this app.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect it in the first place.
- We do not require account creation or ask for your email, name, or any personal identifying information.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds optional cloud sync — we will update this document and, where required, notify users within the app before the change takes effect.
